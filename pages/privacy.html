Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Tools Hub Pro, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

All our 4 offline tools (Word Counter, Case Converter, Password Generator, Stopwatch) run 100% in your browser. We do not collect, store or send your data to any server.

Online tools like QR Generator and Image Compressor process data in your browser where possible. We do not store your images or texts.

If you contact us via our contact form, we only use your email to reply to you. We do not share it with third parties.

We use Bootstrap CDN and Formspree for functionality. Their privacy policies apply.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via our Contact page.
